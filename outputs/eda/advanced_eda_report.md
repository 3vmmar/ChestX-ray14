## 2. Why the hard negatives are hard

The findings a model must separate from pneumonia are frequently printed on the *same image*. A film tagged `Infiltration|Pneumonia` is a positive while one tagged `Infiltration` alone is a negative, and the two can be radiographically indistinguishable. This is a data-level explanation for the difficulty ordering the training curriculum uses.

## 4. Confounds and shortcut risk
A factor that predicts the label without being the pathology is a shortcut the model can learn instead of the disease. Chi-square gives significance; Cramer's V gives effect size, which matters because chi-square grows with sample size alone.

**view_position** - n=11943, chi2=36.35, p=1.647e-09, Cramer's V=0.0552

Positive rate by level: {'AP': 10.02, 'PA': 6.94}

**patient_gender** - n=7068, chi2=8.0, p=0.004685, Cramer's V=0.0336

Positive rate by level: {'F': 6.54, 'M': 8.35}

**patient_age** - median 48.0 (pos) vs 50.0 (neg), Mann-Whitney p=0.001614, Cohen's d=-0.1565
